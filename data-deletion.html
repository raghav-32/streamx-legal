Delete your data: StreamX Studio

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

StreamX Studio has no servers and keeps no copy of your data. Everything it stores (sign-in tokens, stream keys, scenes, overlays, recordings) is on your own phone, so you can delete it all yourself.

Delete from the app and your phone

  1. Open StreamX Studio, go to the More tab and sign out of YouTube and Facebook. This removes the stored tokens.
  2. To remove everything else: Android Settings, Apps, StreamX Studio, Storage, Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  3. Recordings you saved are normal files on your phone; delete them in your gallery or file manager.

Remove the app's access on Facebook

  1. Open Facebook, then Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and websites (or Business Integrations).
  2. Find StreamX Studio, choose Remove, and tick the option to delete posts, videos or events the app created if you want them removed.

Remove the app's access on Google

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose StreamX Studio and select Delete all connections.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/raghav-32/streamx-legal/issues. We hold no personal data on servers, so there is nothing further to erase on our side.
