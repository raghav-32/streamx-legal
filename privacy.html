Privacy Policy: StreamX Studio

App: StreamX Studio (com.streamx.studio) by Nepus Studio. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

StreamX Studio is an Android live-streaming app. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your phone. Video and audio go only to the stream destination you choose (YouTube, Facebook or an RTMP server you enter).

What the app accesses and why

  • Camera and microphone: used only for the preview, streaming and recording after you start them. Recordings are saved on your phone.
  • Screen capture: used only if you start Screen mode and approve Android's capture prompt.
  • Overlays, scenes and images: stored on your phone. A web-page overlay loads the link you enter.
  • Live chat: shown while the app runs and kept only in memory.
  • Stream keys: stored encrypted on your phone and sent only to the server they belong to.

Google and YouTube sign-in

If you sign in with Google, the app requests your basic profile and YouTube access to create and end live broadcasts, upload a thumbnail, read your channel details and read the live chat of your broadcast. Sign-in happens in your browser through Google; the app never sees your password. Tokens are stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore). The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Facebook sign-in

If you sign in with Facebook, the app requests these permissions: public_profile (your name, to show who is signed in), pages_show_list (to list the Pages you manage so you can pick one), pages_read_engagement (to read the live video's status and comments), pages_manage_posts and publish_video (to create and end a live video on the Page or profile you choose). Sign-in uses Facebook Login. The app never sees your password and never holds an app secret. Access tokens are stored encrypted on your phone. Data received from Facebook is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, not shared, not used for advertising and not copied to any server of ours.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No copies of your data on our servers, because we have none.

Your control and deleting your data

  • Sign out of YouTube or Facebook in the app (More tab). This removes the stored tokens from your phone.
  • Revoke access: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook under Settings, Apps and Websites.
  • Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete everything it saved.
  • See how to delete your data for full steps.

Crash information

A short note is saved on your phone if the app crashes. It is shared only if you choose to share it.

Third parties

Google/YouTube, Facebook or the RTMP server you choose process the video you send and your account data under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
